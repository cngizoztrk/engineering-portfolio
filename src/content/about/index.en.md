I am a Mechanical Engineering student who enjoys understanding how engineering systems work beyond their theoretical foundations. Rather than focusing only on final results, I like exploring the reasoning, calculations, and engineering decisions behind a design. I am particularly interested in mechanical design, simulation, finite element analysis, manufacturing, and engineering problem-solving.

Through university projects, technical research, and independent learning, I continuously improve my skills in CAD, engineering analysis, and numerical simulation. My goal is to build practical engineering knowledge by studying real-world problems, documenting what I learn, and developing projects that demonstrate both technical ability and analytical thinking.
